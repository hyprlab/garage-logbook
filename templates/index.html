    Changelog
    Unreleased
    • New: a redesigned dashboard with spending over the last 12 months, spending by type, the reminders coming up next, recent service and every vehicle at a glance
    • New: a top bar on every page with its title, search and main action; the dashboard's time range is picked there instead of in Settings
    • New: service records are added and edited in a panel that slides in from the right, and can be logged from the dashboard by choosing the vehicle
    • New: the theme can follow the system setting, and a theme button sits in the sidebar footer
    • New: each page has its own address, so reloading or bookmarking keeps your place
    • Settings opens as a two-pane window with its sections down the side; on phones it opens as a list
    • The sidebar lists vehicles with their photos and marks the current page with a bar; the version moved to Settings and About
    • The interface uses Inter throughout and fills the width of the window
    • Redesigned About section with the tools Garage Logbook is built with
    0.4.0
    • New: saved vendors. The Vendor field on a service record lists them as you type, and a vendor not on the list can be added from the field without leaving the record
    • New: a Vendors tab in Settings to add, rename and remove saved vendors
    • Vendors already used on your records are added to the list on the first start after updating. Spellings that differ only in capitals, spacing or punctuation become one vendor
    • New: an IMAGE_TAG setting picks the release channel: stable (the default), beta, or a fixed version
    0.3.2
    • New: service reminders can count down time instead of mileage. A toggle picks which, so "brake fluid every 24 months" works alongside "oil every 5,000 miles"
    • Time reminders anchor to the date of the last matching service record, the same way mileage ones anchor to its odometer
    • Adding a service record now lists that vehicle's counters so you can tick which ones to restart from its mileage or date
    • Upcoming Service now sorts mileage and time reminders together by how much of the interval is left
    0.3.1
    • Vehicles now appear in the sidebar under My Garage, so you can jump straight to one
    • Vehicle cards show upcoming service reminders with their mileage countdown
    • Fixed form fields overflowing and being cut off on the right in modals
    0.3.0
    • New: mileage-based service reminders. Set a service interval like "every 30,000 miles" and see what is due
    • Reminders anchor to your service records, so logging the work rolls the next due mileage forward automatically
    • Dashboard now shows a Service Due count and an Upcoming Service list across all vehicles
    • Change Password moved into Settings under a new Account tab, with a confirmation field
    • Sidebar footer simplified: settings is now a gear icon beside sign out
    • Fixed a crash on first launch when running with multiple workers
    0.2.2

    • A failed save no longer locks the database for subsequent saves
